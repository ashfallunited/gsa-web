Youth football in Liberia does not lack passion—it lacks steady support. Ashfall United runs programmes in Monrovia that combine coaching, leadership, health and education links, and a playing style built on total football: controlled, dominant, and disciplined. Our first team is the first in Liberia to compete in the national third division with an all U-18 squad—proof of what investment in young people can produce. Here is how you can help that work continue.

Why support matters now

Every week we train players who might otherwise never see a qualified coach, a safe pitch, or a pathway to national competition. Costs are real: kit, balls, transport, medical support, tournament fees, and staff who show up consistently.

When you support Ashfall United, you fund access—not only highlights.

Donate: keep programmes running

Donations help us:

  • Provide training kit and equipment for academy and first-team players
  • Pay for pitch time, transport, and tournament entry
  • Fund health screenings and basic medical support
  • Subsidise programmes so cost is not a barrier for families in Monrovia
  • Strengthen coaching and leadership education across all age groups

Gifts of any size add up. Visit our donate page to contribute and share the campaign with friends, family, or your organisation.

Volunteer: share your time and skills

Not every supporter can give money—but many can give expertise. We welcome volunteers who can help with:

  • Coaching and goalkeeper training
  • Media, photography, and storytelling
  • Administration, logistics, and event support
  • Mentorship, tutoring, and career guidance
  • Health, nutrition, or wellbeing workshops

If you are in Liberia or abroad, submit an inquiry on Get Involved and tell us how you would like to help. We review every message and respond as quickly as we can.

Partner: scale impact with organisations

Partnerships multiply what we can do. Schools, NGOs, businesses, and diaspora groups partner with Ashfall United to fund programmes, provide services, or co-design projects that fit shared values—sport for social impact, youth leadership, and community development in Monrovia.

Partners often support specific outcomes: academy scholarships, girls' participation, health days, or education supplies. We are open to conversations that respect young people and local leadership.

Other ways to stand with the club

  • Shop — Official merchandise helps fund programmes while spreading the club's name.
  • Share — Follow us on social media and share posts about our U-18 third-division story, academy progress, and community work.
  • Attend — When we announce open sessions or events, show up and cheer for Monrovia's young players.

Transparency and trust

We take responsibility seriously. Support is directed toward programme delivery—coaching, players, and community impact—not overhead for its own sake. As we grow, we continue to document stories and outcomes on our blog and gallery.

What your support builds

When you support Ashfall United, you help build:

  • Young footballers who play total football with control and dominance
  • Leaders who can organise, communicate, and inspire
  • A historic first team pathway that proves Liberian youth can compete nationally
  • A Monrovia where talent meets structure—not by accident, but by design
